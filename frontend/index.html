          Agronomic Weather & Spraying Safety Advisory

          Adhere to international agricultural extension guidelines for high-efficacy treatments and environmental stewardship.

          💧 Humidity & Fungal Risk

          Relative humidity above 85% with temperatures between 18°C-26°C dramatically accelerates Blight, Rust, and Powdery Mildew sporulation. Apply protective fungicides before rainfall periods.

          🌬️ Wind & Spray Drift

          Do not apply chemical sprays when wind speed exceeds 15 km/h to prevent unintended pesticide drift to water sources and non-target beneficial pollinator insects.

          🐝 Pollinator Safety

          Never spray flowering fruit trees or crops during peak midday bee foraging hours. Schedule treatments during early dusk or predawn calm periods.
