          Spraying Weather & Safe Application Guidelines

          Follow safe agricultural practices to maximize treatment effectiveness and protect helpful pollinators.

          💧 Humidity & Fungal Risk

          Relative humidity above 85% accelerates Blight and Powdery Mildew spread. Apply protective organic fungicides early before rain periods.

          🌬️ Wind & Spray Drift

          Do not spray pesticides when wind speed exceeds 15 km/h to prevent spray drift from harming neighboring fields or water sources.

          🐝 Pollinator & Honeybee Safety

          Never spray flowering crops during midday hours when bees are foraging. Always spray during early morning or calm dusk hours.
